# FUTURE_DS_02 – Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn and retention patterns for a telecommunications subscription business.

The goal is to understand:
- Why customers are leaving
- Which customer groups have higher churn
- How long customers typically remain active
- What actions can improve customer retention

## Dataset
Dataset: Telco Customer Churn Dataset

The dataset contains 7,043 customer records and 21 attributes, including:
- Customer information
- Contract type
- Internet service
- Payment method
- Tenure
- Monthly charges
- Churn status

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- Excel formulas
- Data analysis
- Charts and dashboard visualization

## Key Findings
- Overall churn rate is approximately 27%.
- Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 43%.
- Fiber optic customers have a high churn rate of 42%.
- Electronic check users have the highest churn rate at 45%.
- More than half of churned customers left within their first 12 months.
- Churned customers have a lower average tenure than retained customers.
- Churned customers also have higher average monthly charges.

## Recommendations
- Encourage month-to-month customers to move to longer-term contracts.
- Investigate pricing and service issues affecting fiber optic customers.
- Encourage customers to use automatic payment methods.
- Focus retention campaigns on customers during their first year.
- Review pricing and value offered to customers with higher monthly charges.

## Deliverable
The project includes an Excel-based Customer Retention & Churn Analysis dashboard with KPIs, charts, insights, and business recommendations.
